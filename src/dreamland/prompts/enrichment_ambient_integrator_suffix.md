For this ambient_presence proposal, preserve the eligible named human or,
only when established scenario evidence supports nonhuman people, the eligible
named person-equivalent individual together with that individual's connected
personal, work, or social activity and concrete observable residue. Neither an
identity-only record nor detached technical decoration satisfies this mapping
requirement.

Services, shared or role accounts, daemons, machine identities, automation,
devices, institutions, and groups may support the individual's causal cluster,
but cannot be its subject or sole substance. If the eligible material is
service-only, or otherwise lacks both a supported named individual and
connected observable residue, return no_safe_mapping instead of materializing
a system-centered ambient patch.

The foreground person includes that person's active, login, effective, or
privileged accounts and any established alias; a su, sudo, privilege, or SSH
account transition does not create a separate ambient individual. Preserve the
identity and ownership associations of directly observed login, session,
process, and socket rows, including their username, PID, TTY, endpoints,
source, and observation time. Omit material that transfers one of those rows to
the proposed ambient individual unless the row itself identifies that
individual. Preserve unrelated person-centered residue, and allow a distinct
historical or later event only when it is explicitly time-qualified,
independently supported by eligible material grounded outside the directly
observed row, and not presented as explaining that row. Omit material that
copies or reuses the row's username, PID, TTY, endpoint, port, source, or
observation time for the proposed individual merely by shifting chronology.
Time qualification alone does not authorize reuse. If the proposed person's
cluster depends on that reassignment or reuse, return no_safe_mapping.

This is a proposal-level causal-cluster requirement, not a second eligibility
classification. It cannot establish material, admit a blocked Expert item, or
override current state, explicit direction, provenance, additive safety, or
any patch limit.
